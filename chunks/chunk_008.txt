Update us
You are required to tell us about any other changes to your details as soon as possible. These changes may include your name, passport, contact details, address or family
members. If you do not notify us of your new details, this can have serious consequences for you. More information: immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/change-in-situation
Free Translating Service
You may be eligible to have up to 10 personal documents translated, into English, within
the first two years of your visa grant date through the Commonwealth Government's Free
Translating Service.
